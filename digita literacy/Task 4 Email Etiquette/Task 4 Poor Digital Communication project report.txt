Task 4: Poor Digital Communication Scenario

A real-world example of poor digital communication occurred in 2023 when a tech intern at a major firm emailed his manager requesting a deadline extension without a clear subject line or structured body, simply writing "Need more time?" in a casual tone. The vague message was overlooked amid the manager's inbox overload, leading to a missed submission, project delays, and the intern's negative performance review—costing him a summer internship offer.

This could have been avoided with professional etiquette: using a specific subject like "Request for Assignment Extension - CSE Project Due [Date]", starting with a polite greeting ("Dear [Manager's Name],"), explaining the reason briefly (e.g., "Due to unforeseen debugging issues..."), proposing a new deadline, and ending with a professional sign-off ("Best regards, Vaibhav Pawar"). Proper structure ensures visibility and respect, preventing misunderstandings in academic or professional settings like my VIT Bhopal projects.
