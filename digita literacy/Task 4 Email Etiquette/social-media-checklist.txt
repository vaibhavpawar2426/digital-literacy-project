Social Media ChecklistDos:Post academic achievements and projects to build professional presence.Use privacy settings to control audience.Engage positively; tag respectfully.Share credible sources for environmental science or coding tips.Network with peers on LinkedIn for opportunities.Don'ts:Share personal financial details or UPI IDs publicly.Post during class or events; focus on real-time participation.Use slang or emojis in professional groups.React impulsively to controversies; think before posting.Overshare location via bike rides or daily routines
